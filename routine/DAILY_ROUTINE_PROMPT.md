# Daily routine prompt

The routine's full instructions live in the repo as a skill, so they're version-controlled alongside the data they operate on:

**`.claude/skills/daily-coaching-run/SKILL.md`** — the entry point. It references `.claude/skills/generate-day-data/SKILL.md`, `RUNNER_CONTEXT.md`, `DAY_SCORE.md` and `routine/INTERVALS_DATA_REFERENCE.md`.

What you paste into the routine's **Instructions** box is deliberately tiny — three lines — so that editing the routine's behaviour means editing the repo, not the routine. See **`routine/ROUTINE_SETUP.md`** for the exact text and the rest of the form.
