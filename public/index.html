How this is calculated

Everything here is worked out in your browser from a handful of static files: the coaching log, an athlete reference, one wellness row per day, and one full-resolution stream file per training day.

The day score

0 to 100, measuring whether a day moved the odds of the 1:20 goal up (above 60), held them (around 60) or lowered them (below 60). It is scored separately against 1:20 and against the committed 1:24–1:27, and the two can move in opposite directions. It rewards fit-to-purpose rather than effort: an easy run done genuinely easy scores well, and intensity on a day meant to be easy does not. Most days land between 50 and 75.

The projected time

The half-marathon time your current fitness supports — a threshold estimate and 5K conversion, less an endurance discount, cross-checked against the November 2025 time trial of 1:33:42 at CTL 25. It moves at most about 30 seconds a day without a race or a missed week. The probability of actually hitting a goal today rounds to zero all through base phase, so this page shows the gap in minutes instead.

Reading a workout

Your watch stops during standing recoveries, so the gaps in the recorded time are the rep boundaries — the analysis segments on those first, because they are the most reliable description of the session's structure. Auto-lap kilometres are deliberately ignored for this: they straddle rep boundaries and can invent a mid-set collapse that never happened. Each rep is then split where its pace steps, so a mile rep run as 1000 m at 5K pace plus 600 m at mile pace is reported as the two legs it actually was.

Pace, grade and drift

Pace comes from the device velocity stream, smoothed for display and raw for calculation. Grade-adjusted pace uses a Minetti metabolic-cost model calibrated per activity to Intervals.icu's own figure. Splits are computed on moving time so pace and grade-adjusted pace share one clock. Decoupling halves the moving time and compares grade-adjusted speed per heartbeat between the halves — positive means the same speed cost more heart rate later. It only means something on steady efforts of 25 minutes or more.

Easy share

The portion of a week's miles run below 80% of threshold; within a run, the time spent in heart-rate zones 1 and 2. This is the headline number here because the documented failure mode is intensity on days that were meant to be easy.

Zones and cadence

Z1 to 145, Z2 to 153, Z3 to 162, Z4 to 171, Z5 to 176, Z6 to 181, Z7 to 190, from a threshold heart rate of 172 and a maximum of 190. The watch records cadence per leg, so it is doubled for display.

Colour and charts

Series colours come from a documented palette, assigned per measure and validated for colourblind separation and contrast against this page's own background in both light and dark. No chart uses two vertical axes; each has a legend when it plots more than one line, and a button revealing the numbers as a table. GPS traces are never published.
